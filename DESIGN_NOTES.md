# Design Notes — Look Twice

## Creative North Star — Decision Log

### Chosen: "The Second Look"

Selected because it ties the visual metaphor directly to the brand concept: colour
is the dominant surface, B&W photography is revealed underneath through geometric
cutouts. Every layout decision can be checked against it. The brand name earns its
place in the visual logic rather than just the wordmark.

---

### Considered and deferred: "The Bold Diagnosis"

Accurate to the *attitude* of the work - bold, direct, unapologetic - but orients
toward tone rather than a structural visual principle, which makes it harder to
apply consistently to design decisions.

There's also an honest note here: fully living up to "The Bold Diagnosis" visually
requires a level of design confidence and commitment that feels like a stretch right
now. This could be the right North Star for a v2 or rebrand once the positioning is
more established and proven in the market. Worth revisiting when the site evolves.

---

### Considered and set aside: "The Energetic Outsider"

Accurately captures the personality and the professional outsider advantage. But it
is better built through reputation and word of mouth than through a website - it is
what clients come to say about the work, not a design principle. Not commercially
compelling as a visual orientation, and harder to translate into layout decisions.

Could inform copy and tone-of-voice direction rather than visual design.

## Open Exploration Items — Resolve During Build

These are design questions that need real implementation to answer. Don't lock them
in DESIGN.md until they've been tested in the browser.

**Colour system**
- Test whether all twelve colours are genuinely useful on the website specifically,
  or whether some (particularly Link Sage / Link Pine) can be dropped in favour of
  simpler link treatment. Keep only what earns its place.
- Test whether more shadow use brings the site to life, or whether the flat system
  holds up. The current flat-by-default rule is a principle, not a constraint — if
  subtle shadows improve the feel in practice, update the rule.

**Two-mode system**
- Working (Colour Block) mode may be mainly relevant to PPTs, reports, and documents
  rather than the website itself. The website may be primarily Hero mode (drenched
  accent surfaces) with Linen content sections that don't need the full structural
  colour logic of Working mode. Test this in layout — don't assume both modes are
  needed at equal weight on the site.

**Warm vs. cool accents**
- Hot Pink, Signal Orange, Warm Amber are the default warm register.
- Deep Teal, Rich Purple, Cool Indigo are contrasts — used for "looking twice" moments
  (diagnostic reveals, report evidence, contrast sections). These skew toward
  documents and reports more than the site. Use on the site only if they add without
  cluttering. Drop them from the site palette if they create visual noise.

**Buttons**
- Shape (pill, rounded, sharp) was not decided from a rendered example. Explore what
  looks best in context during the build. Don't treat the DESIGN.md radius as fixed.
