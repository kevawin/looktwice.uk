## Vision

looktwice.uk is the website for Look Twice — an independent brand and CX strategy
consultancy run by Kristina (Kris) Evawin. The site is a proof and credibility asset
in a relationship-led BD process. It is not expected to generate cold inbound at
launch. Its job is to be coherent and credible when a warm contact or referral looks
it up.

Visitor journey: arrive from LinkedIn or word of mouth, quickly assess whether Kris
understands their world, decide whether to make contact.

They should leave thinking: "she is describing the problems I have, even if I did not
have the words for them — and I know what my business would get from working with her."

---

## V1 Scope

**Single-page site.** All nav items anchor-scroll to sections on the homepage.
No standalone pages in V1. Go live fast; additional pages are V2.

**What ships in V1:**
- Homepage (full design, all sections)
- Functioning nav with anchor links
- Floating sticky CTA tab
- Email-based contact (no form)
- Responsive (mobile and desktop)
- WCAG AA accessible

**What does NOT ship in V1:**
- Case study detail pages (placeholder section only, content coming)
- Contact form (email link with prompts instead)
- Blog, newsletter, or interactive features
- Standalone About or Work pages

**Primary CTA:** Free 30-45 minute problem identification session. Low commitment.
Entry point: `hello@looktwice.uk` with static copy prompts guiding what to include.

---

## Audience

Decision-makers at SMEs and scale-ups: founders, MDs/CEOs, Heads of Brand/Marketing/CX.
Also: agencies and consultancies needing senior strategy firepower on a project basis.

They arrive already knowing Kris exists. The site validates, it does not cold-sell.

Five situations that define a good fit (these drive the problem identification section):
1. Business has grown faster than the brand — something is off but nobody can name it
2. Inherited a brand strategy they didn't build and don't believe in
3. Operationally excellent but the brand story is weak — premium pricing not sticking
4. Post-rebrand: looks better, nothing changed in the actual experience
5. Agency or consultancy needing senior strategy depth without a full hire

## Brand

**Name:** Look Twice
**Tagline:** Independent brand & customer experience strategy
**Contact:** hello@looktwice.uk
**LinkedIn:** linkedin.com/in/krisevawin
**Domain:** looktwice.uk

**Personality:** Curious, energetic, direct, pragmatic. Confident but not corporate.
Intellectually sharp but not cold. A bit of edge, still professional.

**Voice:** Clear, human, strategically opinionated. Short sentences. Plain English.
No fluff. Witty in small doses — dry or observational, never forced.

**Anti-references (what to avoid):**
- Generic solo brand consultant sites with no personality
- Corporate consultancy tone — no humanity, tries to look clever
- "We help brands grow" filler that says nothing
- Luxury serif, high-polish agency aesthetic
- Stock photography used carelessly
- Identical card grids, decorative motion, heavy scroll burden

## Design North Star

"The Second Look" — colour dominates the surface, B&W photography is revealed
through geometric cutouts. The brand name earns its place in every layout decision.
Look again — something was there that wasn't visible at first. Restraint and
revelation in the same frame.

See DESIGN-TOKENS.md for the full design system.

## Success Criteria

V1 is successful if a warm referral can:

2. Confirm Kris understands their world (not just the category)
3. Know what they'd get from working with her
