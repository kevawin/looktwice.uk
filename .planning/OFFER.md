> **Status:** Locked offer spine (Phase 14). This is the content contract every downstream content phase reads before building. All provisional values carry `[CONFIRM-KRIS]` and await Kris's sign-off.

## Overview

The offer is a three-rung commitment ladder: Diagnose, then Strategy, then Embed. The way in is a free 30-min call, and not every client moves through all three rungs, you start at the rung that fits where you are.

This is a commitment ladder, not a Good/Better/Best tier choice. Each rung is a different kind of engagement for a different stage, not a more expensive version of the same thing.

## Free Call (Entry Point, not a rung)

- **What it is:** a 30-min conversation. Surface-level problem-naming and a quick read on whether there is a fit. No pitch.
- **Duration:** 30 minutes.
- **Purpose:** qualification plus a genuinely useful problem-naming conversation. You leave with a clearer view of your situation whether or not you go further.
- **Reassurance line:** "No sale, no follow-up unless you want one." `[CONFIRM-KRIS]` (established wording, Phase 7 / CLAUDE.md.)
- **Routes to:** Diagnose, or straight to Strategy/Embed when the fit is obvious.
- **Contact route:** the single Formspree contact form at `#contact`. No mailto, no direct booking.

**This is the single primary CTA site-wide.** It is the way in for every rung. It is **not a priced rung**, Diagnose is the first thing a client pays for (D-07/D-08).

**Free call vs the paid Diagnose:** the free call is a 30-min conversation that names the problem at the surface, enough to know whether the Diagnostic is the right next step. Diagnose is a deeper investigation (stakeholder interviews, market research, analysis of current brand communications) with a written report of findings. The free call is discovery, the Diagnostic is investigation.

## Not Cumulative Tiers

These are not cumulative tiers. Each rung is a different kind of engagement for a different stage, not Rung 1 plus Rung 2 plus more. Each has a genuinely different output type. Not every client moves through all three, you start at the rung that fits where you are. This framing is what keeps the offer a commitment ladder rather than a Good/Better/Best pricing table.
